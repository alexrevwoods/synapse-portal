# Mobile full-map destination detail verification

The full-network mobile map now reserves a protected detail zone above the lower controls. When a Node detail is open, its destination card is elevated above the filter rail and minimap; those persistent controls fade out and become non-interactive until the detail is dismissed. This prevents the selected destination content and action button from being obscured.

The closed full-network map was recaptured at 390×844 to verify the clear lower control layout. TypeScript, 13 automated assertions, and the production build pass.
