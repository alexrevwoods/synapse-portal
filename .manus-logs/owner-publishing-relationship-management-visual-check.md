# Owner publishing and relationship management verification

Authenticated mobile and desktop captures of Media Revolution confirm that an owner sees **Publish Signal**, **Manage Portal**, and a Connection-request icon in place of Follow and Connect controls. The owner’s Profile Feed also carries its own **Publish Signal** button, making Signal creation available at both natural entry points. The lower page includes a compact public **Relationship activity** panel that identifies recently accepted follows and Connections with dates and Portal links.

The Timeline now includes stable filters for All, Following, Connections, and My Signals. The relationship actions remain persistent: clicking an active Following or Connected action prompts for confirmation and removes the underlying relationship only after confirmation.

TypeScript compilation, six Vitest files with 13 assertions, the production build, and responsive visual captures passed.
